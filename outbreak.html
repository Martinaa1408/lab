Food outbreak investigation

After a wedding lunch many guests fell ill. Find the dish and the pathogen.

1. The guests

2. Attack rate by dish

3. Incubation and symptoms

Hours from the lunch to the first symptoms.

Method and data

Simulated case. Pathogen profiles follow typical textbook values for incubation and main symptoms. Attack rate = ill / total in each group; relative risk = attack rate among those who ate the dish / attack rate among those who did not. Real investigations add laboratory tests on patients and leftover food.
