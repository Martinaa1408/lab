Lab / Clinical statistics

Clinical calculators

Four scores used every day on hospital wards, with the formula in view and the result explained.

Kidney function (eGFR)

CKD-EPI 2021 equation, without the race coefficient. Units: mL/min/1.73 m². Stages as defined by KDIGO.

Stroke risk in atrial fibrillation (CHA₂DS₂-VASc)

Female sex alone does not indicate anticoagulation. ESC guidance: consider therapy from a score of 2 in men and 3 in women.

Sepsis screening (qSOFA)

A warning tool, not a diagnosis: 2 or more criteria mean higher risk of poor outcome and call for a full SOFA assessment.

Body mass index (BMI)

BMI = weight / height². WHO adult classes; it does not separate fat mass from lean mass.

All calculations run in your browser and no data are sent. For learning only: not a substitute for clinical judgement.
