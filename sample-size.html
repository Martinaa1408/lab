Power by patients per group

Two-sided test, two independent groups of equal size. Proportions: normal approximation (Fleiss, no continuity correction). Means: n = 2(z₁₋α/₂ + z₁₋β)²σ²/Δ². Total = 2n / (1 − drop-out). A real protocol needs a biostatistician.
