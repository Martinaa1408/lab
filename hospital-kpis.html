Method and data
The data are generated by a reproducible random model; no real patient, hospital or client is involved. Occupancy = inpatient days / (beds × 365). Readmission = a new inpatient stay within 30 days of discharge. The SQL refers to a simple star schema with one fact table (fact_admission) and one ward dimension (dim_ward).
