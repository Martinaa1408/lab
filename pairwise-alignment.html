Linear gap penalty; end gaps are penalised in global mode. Ties in the traceback prefer the diagonal. The protein pair is the classic example from Durbin et al., Biological Sequence Analysis (the book scores it with BLOSUM50, so its numbers differ). Scores match Biopython's PairwiseAligner.
