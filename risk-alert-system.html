Turning lab history into risk alerts

A demonstration of the decision support logic from my MSc thesis, on invented data. Each contaminant-food pair gets a score that combines how often it failed in the past with a model's predicted probability, and is placed in a risk tier.

Invented data: contaminant and food names are real categories, every number is simulated and does not come from any laboratory. Score = w·percentile(non-compliance rate) + (1 − w)·percentile(model probability), computed among pairs with enough samples. The real system in my thesis used a gradient-boosted model, statistical enrichment and biological pathway analysis; this page shows only the ranking step.
