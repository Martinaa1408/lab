Is non-compliance really increasing?

Monthly rates jump up and down, so a trend is hard to judge by eye. The Mann-Kendall test asks whether later months tend to be higher than earlier ones, without assuming a straight line or normal data. Sen's slope estimates how fast.

S = Σ sign(xⱼ − xᵢ) over all pairs i < j; variance corrected for ties; Z with continuity correction. Sen's slope = median of all pairwise slopes, intercept = median(x) − slope·(n − 1)/2. Results match the pymannkendall package. Used for seasonal and long-term trends in my MSc thesis.
