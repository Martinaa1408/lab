Predicting the offspring of a cross

Choose the genotypes of two parents for one or two genes. The Punnett square gives the expected proportions; then simulate a real litter and test, with chi-square, whether the counts fit Mendel's ratios.

Punnett square

p-value

Independent assortment is assumed for two genes (no linkage). Chi-square goodness of fit on phenotype classes with expected count > 0; the p-value uses the chi-square distribution with k − 1 degrees of freedom. Example traits from Mendel's pea experiments: Y yellow, y green; R round, r wrinkled. For teaching.
