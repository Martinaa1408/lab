Lab / Food safety

Which foods fail their limits most often?

A food control laboratory runs thousands of analyses. For each contaminant and food pair this tool computes the non-compliance rate with its confidence interval and tests whether it is higher than usual for that contaminant.

Results by pair

Example data are invented, with real contaminant names. 95% Wilson interval. One-sided Fisher exact test for enrichment, comparing the pair with all other foods tested for the same contaminant; Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment; odds ratio with Haldane correction when a cell is zero. This is the inference step of the pipeline in my MSc thesis; results match SciPy and statsmodels.
