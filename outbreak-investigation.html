Lab / Teaching

Who made the wedding guests sick?

A classroom case. After a wedding lunch many guests fell ill. Work like a public health team: compare who ate what, find the dish responsible, then use symptoms and incubation times to name the pathogen.

1. The guest list

Each row is a guest interviewed by the health team: what they ate and whether they fell ill.

2. Attack rates by dish

Attack rate = ill / total in each group. Relative risk = attack rate among those who ate the dish / attack rate among those who did not. Look for a high RR and a low attack rate among those who did not eat it.

3. Incubation and symptoms of the ill guests

Hours from the lunch to the first symptoms (2-hour bins).

Simulated case for teaching; pathogen profiles follow typical textbook values (incubation and main symptoms). Real investigations add laboratory testing of patients and leftover food.
